🔐 Cryptographic Privacy Formulation

Detailed mathematical proofs: Implementing Laplace Differential Privacy ($\epsilon = 0.5$) combined with Merkle Tree auditing.

The probability ratio of generating any output $S$ from adjacent databases $D$ and $D'$ is strictly mathematically bounded at the edge.

$$\text{Pr}[M(D) \in S] \le \exp(\epsilon) \times \text{Pr}[M(D') \in S]$$
