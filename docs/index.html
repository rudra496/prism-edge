Cryptographic Privacy Formulation

Detailed mathematical proofs: Implementing Laplace Differential Privacy ($\epsilon = 0.5$) combined with Merkle Tree auditing to guarantee zero PII reconstruction on untrusted edge nodes.

The probability ratio of generating any output $S$ from adjacent databases $D$ and $D'$ is strictly mathematically bounded at the edge. The formulation below guarantees statistical obfuscation of individual identities before transmission to the Mesh:

$$\text{Pr}[M(D) \in S] \le \exp(\epsilon) \times \text{Pr}[M(D') \in S]$$
$$\text{Lap}(x | \mu, b) = \frac{1}{2b} \exp\left(-\frac{|x - \mu|}{b}\right)$$
