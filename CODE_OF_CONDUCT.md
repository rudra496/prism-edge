# Code of Conduct

PRISM-Edge is a decentralized tool built for the most vulnerable populations on Earth. 

We pledge to making participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Please report unacceptable behavior to the project lead at `rudrasarker130@gmail.com`.
